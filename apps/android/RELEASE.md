MoonCellar for Android opens [mooncellar.space](https://mooncellar.space) as a full-screen app through Chrome (a Trusted Web Activity). New content arrives with the website; a new APK is needed only when the app shell changes.

**Install**

1. Download `mooncellar.apk` below on your phone.
2. Open it and allow installing apps from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Play Protect may warn about an app from outside Google Play — that is expected for an APK from GitHub.

Updating: install the new APK over the old one. Your account and data stay, they live on the server.

Requirements: Android 5.0 or newer with Chrome. Without a browser that supports Trusted Web Activities the site opens in a regular browser tab.
